Privacy, simply.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This website is operated by Albert Garcia. I use optional analytics to understand how people find and use it, and to improve the site.

Your choice

Google Analytics runs by default unless you turn it off. Turning it off does not limit your access to the site. You can change your choice using in the footer. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days; other browsers and devices have their own choices. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page.

Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection and removes this site's accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase information already collected. If Analytics was running, the page refreshes to stop it.

Optional Google Analytics

When enabled, Google Analytics uses cookies (including _ga and cookies beginning with _ga_) and browser identifiers to distinguish visits and sessions. These cookies can last up to two years and may be renewed during use.

  • Information collected includes pages viewed, time and activity on the site, referring websites, browser and device details, and approximate location.
  • Clicks on “Request a meeting” are counted. A click does not mean a meeting was booked, and this tracking does not collect your appointment details.
  • Google processes your IP address to derive approximate location; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store the IP address.

Analytics data is sent to Google and used for site reports, including weekly reports emailed to me. This setup does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or phone numbers to Analytics. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google Analytics data safeguards.

Hosting and other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process technical connection information to deliver and secure websites, independently of optional analytics. The professional page also loads fonts from Google Fonts. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google Fonts privacy information.

If you follow a meeting, video, social, or other external link, the destination service has its own privacy practices. Sending me an email or requesting a meeting shares the information you provide so I can respond.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests about information you have shared with me, contact me@al-garcia.com.
