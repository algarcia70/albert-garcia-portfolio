How to play

Continental is played over eight hands. Build the combination shown for each hand, then get rid of all your cards. The lowest total score wins the match.

7♠7♥7♣
Trío

Three or more cards of the same rank.

4♥5♥6♥7♥
Escalera

Four or more consecutive cards in the same suit.

A♥ and A♦ are wild. A trío needs at least two natural cards of the same rank. An escalera needs at least three natural cards of the same suit.

Set up and take a turn

Play with at least three people. Use two decks for three or four players. With more players, use one deck for every two people, rounding up.

Deal 11 cards to each player. Turn one card face up to start the discard pile. Leave the rest face down as the draw pile.

On your turn, take the top card from either pile, then discard one card. Play passes clockwise.

If you pass on the top discard, another player may take it before you draw from the face-down pile. That player must also take a penalty card from the face-down pile. You then draw and finish your turn.

Lay down your cards and go out

Once you have the required combination, you may lay it down on your turn and keep any extra cards in your hand. Once you have laid down, you can play extra cards on your turn onto other players’ combinations once they have laid them down.

The hand ends when someone has no cards left. If the very first player to lay down goes out immediately with no cards left, it’s a doublette. Everyone else doubles the points for the cards still in their hands.

Count the points

The winner scores zero. Everyone else adds up the cards still in their hands. Enter the original points and select Double Win for a doublette.
